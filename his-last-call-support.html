How can we help?

For questions about His Last Call, technical problems, purchases, accessibility, or privacy, contact us using our support form.

Open support form

The form is hosted by Google and may still show the earlier Zooli name. Please include “His Last Call” in your message.

Reporting a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, game version, language, chapter, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or private Apple account information.

Purchases

If you have already purchased the chapter unlock, use Settings → Unlock / restore purchase → Restore purchases with the same Apple account. Restoring purchases restores access to paid chapters; it does not itself restore story progress. If that does not resolve the issue, contact support.

Saving and privacy

Progress is saved on your device. Keep the app installed while we investigate a save problem. If iCloud synchronization is available in your installed version, it depends on your Apple account and device settings.

Read our privacy policy for information about saved progress and Apple services.
