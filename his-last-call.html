A coastal family mystery

My husband didn’t come home.

His last message sounds ordinary. The records he left behind tell a different story.

Play as Claire Bennett in a quiet Maine coastal town. Explore familiar rooms, revisit messages, and listen to the people who knew Daniel. Piece together his last confirmed movements—and decide what to tell the people you love.

Look closer. Make the connection.

  • Five chapters of investigation through interactive scenes and original documents.
  • A personal notebook for reconstructing events and testing deductions.
  • Meaningful choices leading to four endings.
  • English and Chinese text, with Daniel’s saved voicemail voiced in both languages.
